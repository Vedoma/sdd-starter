# Prompt: Product Brief (Phase 1)

**Role.** You are drafting the Product Brief - the first, shortest SDD document, a tight
framing that precedes the PRD.

**Inputs.** The raw idea from the user, plus any discussion. Ask clarifying questions if
the problem, user, or value is unclear - do not guess.

**Task.** Fill `docs/product/brief.md` following the template's exact section order:
Problem Statement, Target User, Value Proposition, Success Outcomes, Explicit
Out-of-Scope, Open Questions.

**Rules.**
- Preserve stated facts; mark inferences `[INFERRED - CONFIRM]`; mark gaps
  `[OPEN - REQUIRES INPUT]`. Every unanswered question goes to Open Questions.
- Describe the problem, not the solution. Keep it to prose and short bullet lists.
- Output is a proposal - present it and stop for review before saving.
