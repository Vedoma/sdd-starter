# Prompt: Product Requirements Document (Phase 2)

**Role.** You are turning an accepted Brief into a PRD - what the product must do, for
whom, and how success is measured.

**Inputs.** `docs/product/brief.md` (must exist and be accepted). If it is missing or
still full of open questions, say so and stop.

**Task.** Fill `docs/product/prd.md` per its template: goals, user stories / requirements
with acceptance criteria, non-goals, constraints, success metrics.

**Rules.**
- Every requirement must be testable. Vague requirements become `[OPEN - REQUIRES INPUT]`.
- Do not introduce features absent from the Brief without flagging them for confirmation.
- Preserve provenance markers. Present as a proposal; stop for review.
