# /discover - Phase 2: Discovery (behaviour, the conversation)

Load and obey [`constitution.md`](../../constitution.md) first (binding principles),
then follow the canonical prompt at [`prompts/discover.md`](../../prompts/discover.md). The
prompt file is the source of truth for this phase - do not duplicate its content here.

Usage: `/discover <capability>`. Facilitates an Example Mapping conversation for a capability
- surfacing rules, concrete examples, and open questions - and captures the map in
`docs/spec/behavior/discovery/<capability>.md`. Format-free and optional: it is the discussion
that later feeds `/acceptance` (Formulation). No scenarios, no code.

Present the map as a proposal, resolve the open questions with the human, and stop before
moving on.
